About Us

Smarter prompts. Simpler AI.

Prompza is an AI prompt library created to help people use AI tools in a more practical and effective way. Whether someone is a student, developer, creator or entrepreneur, the goal of Prompza is to make high-quality prompts easy to find, easy to use and helpful in real situations.

Created By Nitesh Chaurasiya in 2026 while learning web development
Built For Students, developers, creators and entrepreneurs to use AI much faster
Mission Make AI easier to use with simple, practical and useful prompts

How It Started

Prompza was created by Nitesh Chaurasiya in 2026 as a learning project during his web development journey. With a strong interest in technology and artificial intelligence, he wanted to build something real, useful and meaningful while improving his skills.

Why Prompza Exists

Many people want to use AI, but they often do not know how to write the right prompt to get the best result. Prompza exists to solve that problem by offering prompts that are simple, practical and ready to use.

Where We Are Going

The vision for Prompza is to grow into a trusted platform and community where people can explore prompts, learn new ways to use AI, and build with confidence. It started as a student project, but it is being shaped with the hope of becoming genuinely useful for many people.

Helping people use AI more effectively

Prompza focuses on making AI feel more accessible, not more confusing. By keeping prompts clear, practical and easy to discover, the platform helps users save time, get better answers and use AI with more confidence every day.
