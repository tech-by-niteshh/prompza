1. Introduction

At Prompza, we respect your privacy and believe in being clear about how information is handled on our website. Prompza is an AI prompt library platform designed to help users discover and use useful AI prompts in a simple and practical way. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and how advertising such as Google AdSense may work on the site.

2. Information Collection

Prompza does not automatically collect personal information such as your name or email address just by visiting the website. You can browse the platform without directly giving us personal details. We only receive personal information such as your name, email address, and message content if you choose to contact us through the Contact Us page or submit a request form.

Like most websites, Prompza may collect limited non-personal information automatically for technical and analytics purposes. This may include information such as browser type, device type, pages visited, time spent on the website, and IP address. This type of information is used to help the website run properly and improve the user experience.

3. How We Use Data

Any personal information you provide through the Contact Us page or request forms is only used to respond to your message, process your request, or provide support. We do not sell or trade your personal information.

Non-personal information may be used to improve website performance, understand how visitors use the site, fix technical issues, and help keep the website secure. Our goal is to keep Prompza useful, reliable, and easy to use.

4. Google AdSense

Prompza may use Google AdSense to show advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your visit to this website and other websites. These cookies help display ads that may be more relevant to you.

Google may collect and process data according to its own privacy and advertising policies. If required in your region, consent options for ads and cookies may be shown when you use the website. You can also learn more about how Google uses data through Google’s Privacy Policy and ad settings.

5. Consent

By using Prompza, you agree to this Privacy Policy and the practices described here. If you choose to use the Contact Us page or submit a request, you consent to providing the information needed for that purpose. If you do not agree with this policy, you should stop using the website.

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time if the website changes or if new requirements apply. Any updates will be posted on this page so users can stay informed.
